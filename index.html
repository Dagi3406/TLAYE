May the Lord who was baptized in the Jordan at ጥምቀት and who carried the Cross for us at መስቀል keep you in His peace all the days of your life. May እመቤታችን ማርያም spread her white ነጠላ of protection over you, and may ቅዱስ ሚካኤል and ቅዱስ ገብርኤል walk beside you on every road you take.

Since 24 · 04 · 2026 my days have had a different colour. Thank you for every laugh, every quiet moment, and every little thing you do that makes my world softer and brighter.

May your life be filled with ሰላም — peace, ፍቅር — love, and በረከት — blessing. May this year bring you closer to everything you wish for, and to the One who gives every good gift.

Happy birthday, my love. Here's to us, and to every birthday after this one. 🎂
